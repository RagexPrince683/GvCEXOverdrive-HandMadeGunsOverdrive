## Supplied Minecraft images

I inspected all 13 images in the supplied `Military ref` folder. Several show how a dark bay reads beneath a lighter roof edge, how service paving follows doors, and how narrow circulation can coexist with larger open work areas. These details informed the existing GVCO plots at their current scale.

- `18406612_l.webp`, `18407630_l.webp`, `8c92a833a6e2b39bc5e7621ba0e89bf1.webp`, and `9f867a78a30571c0c28cd273c4d6d54b.webp` show segmented curved roofs and strongly framed openings. Those shapes informed the barrel profiles and bay faces.
- `2013-12-21_001006_6849834.jpg`, `7lgppfvicfsg1.webp`, and `19233566_l.webp` show industrial aprons, repeated door bays, ribbed roofs, and visible utility structures. Those cues informed Company yard and depot frontage.
- `Greenville1.png`, `Greenville2.png`, `Greenvill3.png`, `19951810-pack_l.webp`, and the two `picsart_09-04` images show contrasts between residential rhythm, low service wings, and larger administrative masses. Those contrasts informed the height and frontage of buildings within existing plots.
