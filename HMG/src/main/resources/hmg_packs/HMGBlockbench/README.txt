HMG Blockbench Guns
===================

This pack owns the pre-existing experimental Blockbench AKM presentation and its
pack-local dependencies. It was moved out of GVCguns so imported-model test content
does not appear in the default legacy gun tab.

The item keeps the original HMG AKM gameplay values, ammunition, attachments,
firing audio, and registry ID. Its Gun Smithing Table recipe inherits the exact
legacy AKM recipe through CopyRecipe.

Minecraft was not launched as part of this organization pass.
