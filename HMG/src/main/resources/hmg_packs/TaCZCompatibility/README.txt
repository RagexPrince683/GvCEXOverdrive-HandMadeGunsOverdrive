TaCZ native Bedrock compatibility validation pack

The AK-47 and Glock 17 geometry, animation, model-texture, and item-icon files in
this directory are byte-for-byte copies of the official TaCZ default-gun assets.
They were not converted or edited. The HMG gun TXT files are new compatibility
definitions and do not import TaCZ gameplay.

Original project: Timeless and Classics Zero (TaCZ)
Project: https://github.com/MCModderAnchor/TACZ
Artists credited by the project: NekoCrane, Receke, Pos_2333

official pack. Keep the copied presentation assets unchanged.
