Official TaCZ presentation assets
=================================

Source: Timeless and Classics Zero (TaCZ), including its bundled default gun pack.
Project: Serene Wave Studio / Timeless Squad.

Credited contributors identified by the upstream project and supplied source inventory:
286799714; TartaricAcid; F1zeiL; xjqsh; ClumsyAlien; NekoCrane; Receke;
Pos_2333; Argon4W; Exopandora; 3TUSK.

HMG-Overdrive uses byte-for-byte copies of the selected TaCZ geometry, model texture,
animation, and dependency-selected non-firing mechanical audio resources. The 2D item icons and scope overlays under textures/items
and textures/misc are byte-for-byte copies from the original HMG GVCguns and
Addfixing packs; they are retained dependencies of the migrated HMG definitions,
not TaCZ assets. HMG gameplay definitions, ballistics, magazines, attachments, fire
sounds, and authority remain HMG-owned. Inclusion does not imply endorsement of
HMG-Overdrive by TaCZ or any contributor.

The upstream README.txt and LICENSE.txt are retained beside this notice.
