Imported ClassicRCCRP assets are the selected AK-74, AK-74M, AKS-74U, HK416,
M110, and MG36 geometry, model textures, weapon-local animations, and the
non-firing mechanical sounds referenced by their imported animation markers.
The extracted source pack does not provide more specific per-asset model,
texture, animation, or audio author fields, so the packmaker is credited for
each category rather than inventing authorship.

The shared rifle_default animation copied into this pack comes from official
TaCZ (Serene Wave Studio / Timeless Squad) and remains covered by the retained
TaCZ GPL-3.0 license text in LICENSE_TaCZ_GPL-3.0.txt. The AKS-74U uses
ClassicRCCRP's rifle_tac_rush_default instead.

The 2D item icons and scope overlays under textures/items and textures/misc are
unchanged dependencies from HMG's GVCguns pack. HMG gameplay definitions,
ballistics, recoil, spread, fire rate, magazines, ammunition, attachment rules,
reload authority, and firing sounds remain HMG-owned. The compatibility changes
are limited to pack-local paths, separate item registrations, imported-model
directives, and animation-marker mechanical audio. Inclusion does not imply
endorsement by ClassicRCCRP, Cima Arms Modification Group, TaCZ, or contributors.
